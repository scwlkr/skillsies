# Visual tldraw

Make the idea clear at a glance. These are style preferences, not a fixed template.

- Show the idea: sketches for screens, paths for journeys, timelines for plans, connectors for relationships. Choose what fits.
- Use short titles and labels. Move supporting prose into an accompanying document when useful; preserve important meaning and decisions.
- Give each area a clear focal point, readable text, breathing room, and a small purposeful color palette. Avoid walls of text and menus of similar cards.
- Add personality with relevant icons, illustrations, and the project's own artwork. Let the content and brand guide the look.
- Prefer editable native shapes, text, and groups. Leave room for sticky notes and preserve existing feedback.
- Adapt the pages and layout to the task. No required page count, word quota, or particular visual form.

Use the user's chosen tldraw app and format. For offline files, embed needed assets. Open the saved result, check readability and clipping, and confirm it survives saving and reopening.
